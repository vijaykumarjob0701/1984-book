## Summary

...lectual inferiority. He watches the heavy yet graceful form strolling to and fro, in and out of the range of his vision. O'Brien is a being in all ways larger than himself. There is no idea that he has ever had, or could have, that O'Brien has not long ago known, examined, and rejected. His mind *contained* Winston's mind. But in that case how could it be true that O'Brien was mad? It must be he, Winston, who was mad. O'Brien halts and looks down at him. His voice has grown stern again. Do not imagine that you will save yourself, Winston, however completely you surrender to us. No one who has once gone astray is ever spared. And even if we chose to let you live out the natural term of your life, still you would never escape from us. What happens to you here is for ever. Understand that in advance. We shall crush you down to the point from which there is no coming back. Things will happen to you from which you could not recover, if you lived a thousand years. Never again will you be capable of ordinary human feeling. Everything will be dead inside you. Never again will you be capable of love, or friendship, or joy of living, or laughter, or curiosity, or courage, or integrity. You will be hollow. We shall squeeze you empty, and then we shall fill you with ourselves. He pauses and signs to the man in the white coat. Winston is aware of some heavy piece of apparatus being pushed into place behind his head. O'Brien has sat down beside the bed, so that his face is almost on a level with Winston's. "Three thousand," he says, speaking over Winston's head to the man in the white coat. Two soft pads, which feel slightly moist, clamp themselves against Winston's temples. He quails. There is pain coming, a new kind of pain. O'Brien lays a hand reassuringly, almost kindly, on his. This time it will not hurt. Keep your eyes fixed on mine. At this moment there is a devastating explosion, or what seemed like an explosion, though it was not certain whether there was any noise. There is undoubtedly a blinding flash of light.
