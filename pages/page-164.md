## Summary

...writing-table with its green-shaded lamp and the speakwrite and the wire baskets deep-laden with papers. The incident is closed. Within thirty seconds, it occurs to Winston, O'Brien will be back at his interrupted and important work on behalf of the Party. The page is short: the Inner Party room snaps back into official business the moment the visitors are gone. Chapter 8 ends on that picture — the lamp, the speakwrite, the loaded baskets — and on Winston's sense that the conspiracy meeting is already being filed away so the Party's ordinary work can resume.
