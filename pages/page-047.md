## Summary

Chapter 5 opens in a low, noisy underground canteen. The lunch queue crawls forward. Steam from stew has a sour metallic smell that does not quite hide Victory Gin; a hole-in-the-wall bar sells large nips for ten cents. A voice behind Winston belongs to Syme from Research — not quite a "friend" (people have comrades now), but better company than most. Syme is a philologist working on the Eleventh Edition of the Newspeak Dictionary: tiny, dark-haired, with large staring eyes that are both sad and mocking. He asks for razor blades. Winston says he has none and that they no longer exist, though he is secretly hoarding two unused blades. For months some everyday article has been missing from Party shops — buttons, darning wool, shoelaces, now blades — and you can get them only by furtive scrounging on the "free" market. Winston untruthfully adds that he has used the same blade for six weeks. The queue jerks on; each man takes a greasy metal tray.
