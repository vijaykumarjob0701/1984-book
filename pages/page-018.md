## Summary

The woman hides her face; she seems to be praying. Then the whole group chants "B-B!" slowly and heavily, like a savage hymn mixed with imagined drums and stamping feet. The chant is both praise of Big Brother and self-hypnosis: rhythmic noise used to drown thought. Winston's guts go cold. He cannot help joining the Hate's frenzy, but this chant horrifies him. He still chants, because hiding your feelings and copying the crowd is instinct. For a couple of seconds his eyes might have given him away — and that is when the important thing happens, if it happens at all. He meets O'Brien's eye as O'Brien stands and resettles his glasses. In a fraction of a second Winston is sure they share the same thought. An unspoken message seems to pass: O'Brien knows Winston's contempt and is on his side. Then the look is gone and O'Brien's face is as unreadable as anyone else's. Winston is already unsure it occurred. Such moments never lead anywhere; they only keep alive the hope that he is not the Party's only enemy.
