## Summary

Winston tries to remember old London: houses propped with timber, windows covered in cardboard, sagging garden walls, bomb sites full of dust and willow-herb, and cheap wooden huts like chicken-houses. He cannot. Childhood comes back only as bright, confusing pictures with no context. The Ministry of Truth (Minitrue in Newspeak) is a shining white pyramid 300 metres high, totally unlike the slums around it. On its face are the Party's three slogans: war is peace, freedom is slavery, ignorance is strength. The building is said to have thousands of rooms above ground and matching spaces below. Three other giant ministries of the same size can all be seen from Victory Mansions. Together they run the whole government: Truth (news, entertainment, education, arts), Peace (war), Love (law and order), and Plenty (the economy). Their Newspeak names are Minitrue, Minipax, Miniluv, and Miniplenty.
