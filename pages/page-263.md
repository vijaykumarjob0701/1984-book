## Summary

The unnamed things hover near his face. Gin rises; he has grown fatter and redder, even the bald scalp too pink. A waiter brings chessboard and *The Times* opened at the problem, then more gin. His table is reserved; nobody sits near him. Bills look too small; it would not matter. He has money now, and a well-paid sinecure. The telescreen cuts the music for a Ministry of Plenty note: bootlace quota overfulfilled. He sets out a tricky knight ending: white to play and mate in two. Looking at Big Brother, he thinks it is always so arranged — white always mates; black never wins. The huge face gazes back, calm. Then a graver voice: stand by for an important announcement at fifteen-thirty.
