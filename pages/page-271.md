## Summary

He is walking in sunlight with an armed guard at his back. The long-hoped-for bullet is entering his brain. Then he is in the café again, gazing at the enormous face. Forty years, he thinks, to learn what kind of smile hid under the moustache. The old resistance now looks like a cruel, needless misunderstanding — stubborn exile from the loving breast. Two gin-scented tears run down his nose. It is all right. The struggle is finished. He has won the victory over himself. He loved Big Brother. The novel proper ends here, under the words THE END.
