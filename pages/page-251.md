## Summary

Not being beaten, having food, and being clean is enough; he is not bored. Slowly he spends less time asleep and checks that his muscles really are filling out — thighs thicker than knees again. He starts exercising, then is humiliated by how little he can do: no run, no stool held out, no one-legged stand. Squats and press-ups come back meal by meal. Pride in the body returns until his hand finds the bald scalp and he remembers the ruined face in the mirror. His mind wakes. On the slate he sets about re-educating himself. He has capitulated — in fact he was ready to, even in the moment of arrest. Against the Party, his rebellion now looks shallow.
