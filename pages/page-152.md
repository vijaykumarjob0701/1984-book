## Summary

He thinks for a little while. Has it ever occurred to her that the best thing would be simply to walk out of here before it is too late, and never see each other again? Yes, dear, several times. But she is not going to do it all the same. They have been lucky, he says, but it cannot last much longer. She is young. She looks normal and innocent. If she keeps clear of people like him, she might stay alive another fifty years. No. She has thought it all out. What he does, she is going to do. And he should not be too downhearted: she is rather good at staying alive. They may be together another six months — a year — there is no knowing. At the end they are certain to be apart. Does she realise how utterly alone they will be? Once the Party gets hold of them there will be nothing, literally nothing, that either can do for the other. If he confesses, they will shoot her; if he refuses, they will shoot her just the same. Nothing he can do or say, or stop himself from saying, will put off her death by as much as five minutes. Neither will even know whether the other is alive or dead. They will be utterly without power of any kind. The one thing that matters is that they should not betray one another, although even that cannot make the slightest difference. If he means confessing, she says, they will do that, right enough. Everybody always confesses. You cannot help it. They torture you. He does not mean confessing. Confession is not betrayal. What you say or do does not matter: only feelings matter. If they could make him stop loving her — that would be the real betrayal. She thinks it over. They cannot do that, she says finally. It is the one thing they cannot do. They can make you say anything — anything — but they cannot make you believe it. They cannot get inside you. No, he says a little more hopefully: that is quite true. They cannot get inside you. If you can feel that staying human is worth while, even when it cannot have any result whatever, you have beaten them. He thinks of the telescreen with its never-sleeping ear.
