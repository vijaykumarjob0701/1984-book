## Summary

He wonders whether a microphone is hidden nearby. They have spoken only in low whispers, so it would not catch their words, but it would catch the thrush. Perhaps at the other end some small beetle-like man is listening to that. By degrees the flood of music drives speculation out. It is like liquid poured over him, mixed with the filtered sunlight. He stops thinking and merely feels. Her waist in his arm is soft and warm. He pulls her breast to breast; her body seems to melt into his. Wherever his hands move it is as yielding as water. Their mouths cling; it is quite different from the hard kisses earlier. When they move apart both sigh deeply. The bird takes fright and flees with a clatter of wings. He puts his lips to her ear: "NOW." Not here, she whispers — back to the hide-out; it is safer. They thread their way back, twigs crackling. Inside the ring of saplings she turns, both breathing fast, the smile back at the corners of her mouth. She looks at him, then feels at the zipper of her overalls. And yes — it is almost as in his dream. Almost as swiftly as he imagined, she tears her clothes off and flings them aside with that same magnificent gesture by which a whole civilization seemed to be annihilated. Her body gleams white in the sun. For a moment he does not look at it; his eyes are anchored by the freckled face and its faint, bold smile. He kneels and takes her hands. Has she done this before? Of course. Hundreds of times — well, scores of times, anyway. With Party members? Yes, always. With members of the Inner Party?
