## Summary

The missing end of the sentence is the intoxication of power, always growing finer. If you want a picture of the future, imagine a boot stamping on a human face — for ever. The face will always be there to stamp; heretics are needed so they can be crushed again. Arrests, torture, and Goldstein's heresies will never stop. The seven-year drama with Winston will be replayed, subtler each generation, until the heretic crawls to their feet of his own accord. Winston finds a voice: you cannot make that world; it is a dream; it is impossible. O'Brien asks why.
