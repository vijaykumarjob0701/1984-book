## Summary

The Ministry must also repeat the whole operation at a lower level for the proletariat. Separate departments make prole literature, music, drama, and entertainment: trashy papers of sport, crime, and astrology; sexy five-cent novelettes; sex-heavy films; and sentimental songs composed mechanically on a kaleidoscope-like "versificator." A sub-section called Pornosec produces the lowest pornography, sent in sealed packets; no Party member except its staff may look at it. Three more simple messages arrive and Winston finishes them before the Two Minutes Hate. Afterwards he gets out the Newspeak dictionary, clears his glasses, and settles to the main morning job. Work is his greatest pleasure. Most of it is dull, but some tasks are so intricate you can lose yourself in them — delicate forgeries guided only by Ingsoc and a guess at what the Party wants. He is good at this and has even rewritten *Times* leaders in Newspeak. He unrolls the held-over slip about Big Brother's December day-order being extremely unsatisfactory.
