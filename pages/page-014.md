## Summary

Emmanuel Goldstein, called the Enemy of the People, appears on the screen and the room hisses. The sandy-haired woman makes a sound of fear and disgust. Goldstein is said to have been a Party leader almost equal to Big Brother, then a counter-revolutionary sentenced to death who escaped. Every Two Minutes Hate stars him as the first traitor; later crimes are treated as growing from his ideas. Rumours place him abroad under foreign pay, or even hidden in Oceania. Winston's chest tightens. Goldstein's thin, bearded, sheep-like face and voice fill him with mixed pain. The speech is a venomous, over-the-top attack on Party doctrine — so extreme a child should see through it, yet just believable enough to worry Winston that weaker minds might agree. Goldstein insults Big Brother, demands peace with Eurasia, and calls for speech, press, assembly, and thought. He says the revolution was betrayed, in a rapid, many-syllabled style that copies and mocks Party orators, packed with extra Newspeak.
