## Summary

The comparison continues: know the official god, and every other name is simply "false." In the same way a Party member knows right conduct in vague terms. Sex is run by two words. SEXCRIME covers every sexual "misdeed," including ordinary pleasure for its own sake; GOODSEX is intercourse in marriage for children only, without pleasure on the woman's part. Ordinary people need no finer list. In Newspeak you can rarely follow a heretical thought past noticing that it *is* heretical: the next words are missing. No B-word is politically neutral. Many are euphemisms that mean the opposite of their look: JOYCAMP is a forced-labour camp; MINIPAX is the Ministry of War. PROLEFEED is the cheap entertainment and fake news fed to the masses. Other words flip good/bad depending on whether they apply to the Party or its enemies. Many look like mere shortenings, but their shape is the ideology.
