## Summary

...commonplace, mean-looking man who might have been an engineer or technician of some kind. But what is startling is the emaciation of his face. It is like a skull. Because of its thinness the mouth and eyes look disproportionately large, and the eyes seem filled with a murderous, unappeasable hatred of somebody or something. The man sits down on the bench at a little distance from Winston. Winston does not look at him again, but the tormented, skull-like face is as vivid in his mind as though it had been straight in front of his eyes. Suddenly he realizes what is the matter. The man is dying of starvation. The same thought seems to occur almost simultaneously to everyone in the cell. There is a very faint stirring all the way round the bench. The eyes of the chinless man keep flitting towards the skull-faced man, then turning guiltily away, then being dragged back by an irresistible attraction. Presently he begins to fidget on his seat. At last he stands up, waddles clumsily across the cell, digs down into the pocket of his overalls, and, with an abashed air, holds out a grimy piece of bread to the skull-faced man. There is a furious, deafening roar from the telescreen. The chinless man jumps in his tracks. The skull-faced man has quickly thrust his hands behind his back, as though demonstrating to all the world that he refuses the gift. "Bumstead!" roars the voice. "2713 Bumstead J.! Let fall that piece of bread!" The chinless man drops the piece of bread on the floor. Remain standing where you are, says the voice. Face the door. Make no movement. The chinless man obeys. His large pouchy cheeks are quivering uncontrollably. The door clangs open. As the young officer enters and steps aside, there emerges from behind him a short stumpy guard with enormous arms and shoulders. He takes his stand opposite the chinless man, and then, at a signal from the officer, lets free a frightful blow, with all the weight of his body behind it, full in the chinless man's mouth. The force of it seems almost to knock him clear of the floor. His body is flung across the cell and fetches up against the base of the lavatory seat.
