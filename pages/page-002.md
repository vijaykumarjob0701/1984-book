## Summary

This page is a short life of Eric Arthur Blair, who wrote under the name George Orwell. It follows him from a childhood in British India and school at Eton, through service in the Indian Imperial Police, poorly paid writing years in Paris and England, and books about poverty and empire. It notes his marriage to Eileen O'Shaughnessy, his fighting in the Spanish Civil War, and how the Communist purges in Barcelona pushed him toward anti-Stalinist democratic socialism. Later he worked on wartime radio propaganda at the BBC, published *Animal Farm*, wrote *Nineteen Eighty-Four* (1949), and married Sonia Brownell shortly before he died.
