## Summary

EQUAL no longer carries "politically equal"; that extra sense has been purged. In 1984, while people still know Oldspeak, someone might remember the old meanings, but doublethink handles that, and in two generations the risk vanishes. A native Newspeak speaker would no more know that FREE once meant intellectually free than a person who had never heard of chess would know the extra meanings of QUEEN and ROOK. Nameless crimes become unimaginable. Over time the language is to grow narrower and more rigid. When Oldspeak is gone, the last link with the past snaps. Old books, even if scraps survive, will be unreadable. You cannot translate Oldspeak into Newspeak unless the passage is technical, everyday, or already orthodox (GOODTHINKFUL). In practice no book from before about 1960 could be translated as a whole. Older literature would get an "ideological translation" — sense changed as well as words. The page ends by introducing a famous passage from the Declaration of Independence.
