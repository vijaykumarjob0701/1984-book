## Summary

When *The Times* calls an orator a DOUBLEPLUSGOOD DUCKSPEAKER, that is a warm compliment. The C vocabulary is extra: scientific and technical terms, rigidly defined, following the same grammar. Almost none of it belongs in everyday or political talk. A technician knows his own list and little of the others. There is no general word for Science as a way of thinking; INGSOC already covers any meaning it might have. Unorthodox opinions above a crude level are nearly impossible. You could say BIG BROTHER IS UNGOOD, but you could not argue it, because the tools are missing. Hostile ideas can exist only as a vague blur, or by cheating — translating words back into Oldspeak. ALL MANS ARE EQUAL is a possible sentence, but only as a silly untruth about size or strength, like saying all men are red-haired.

## Difficult vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| supplementary | extra, sitting beside the other lists |
| smattering | a tiny bit of knowledge |
| well-nigh | almost |
| blasphemy | a crude insult against the official "god" |
| inimical | hostile; working against |
| illegitimately | by breaking the language's own rules |
| palpable | obvious to the touch or eye |

## Difficult phrases

| Phrase | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| "DOUBLEPLUSGOOD DUCKSPEAKER" | highest praise for a mindless orthodox orator |
| "no word for 'Science'" | scientific method as a habit of mind is absorbed into INGSOC |
| "BIG BROTHER IS UNGOOD" | sayable, but not defendable in Newspeak |
| "ALL MANS ARE EQUAL" | grammar is fine; political equality has been deleted from EQUAL |
