## Summary

Wind spins dust and paper in the street. The sky is a hard blue, but almost everything looks colourless except the Big Brother posters on every corner. One torn poster flaps and shows the word INGSOC. A police helicopter dips between roofs like a fly and peers into windows; Winston thinks ordinary patrols matter less than the Thought Police. The telescreen keeps talking about pig-iron and a Three-Year Plan. It both sends and receives: anything louder than a whisper, and any movement in its view, can be heard or seen. Nobody knows when they are watched, so people live as if every sound and daytime movement is recorded. Winston faces away from the screen. In the distance stands the huge white Ministry of Truth. He realises this grim city is London, capital of Airstrip One in Oceania, and he cannot remember whether London was always this ruined.
