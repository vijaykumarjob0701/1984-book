## Summary

To feel that *Times* line you must already know INGSOC, BELLYFEEL (blind, gut-level enthusiasm), and OLDTHINK (tied to wickedness and decay). Some Newspeak words exist less to say things than to wipe other words out. Once a blanket term covers a whole battery of older words, those words can be scrapped. Dictionary-makers struggle not to invent terms but to decide what each new term *cancels*. FREE is kept only after heretical senses are purged. HONOUR, JUSTICE, MORALITY, DEMOCRACY, SCIENCE, RELIGION and the like simply vanish. Liberty and equality collapse into CRIMETHINK; objectivity and rationalism into OLDTHINK. Precision would be dangerous. A Party member needs only the outlook of someone who knows "false gods" without needing their names.
