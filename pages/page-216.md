## Summary

...his mouth and nose. A very faint whimpering or squeaking, which seems unconscious, comes out of him. Then he rolls over and raises himself unsteadily on hands and knees. Amid a stream of blood and saliva, the two halves of a dental plate fall out of his mouth. The prisoners sit very still, their hands crossed on their knees. The chinless man climbs back into his place. Down one side of his face the flesh is darkening. His mouth has swollen into a shapeless cherry-coloured mass with a black hole in the middle of it. From time to time a little blood drips onto the breast of his overalls. His grey eyes still flit from face to face, more guiltily than ever, as though he were trying to discover how much the others despise him for his humiliation. The door opens. With a small gesture the officer indicates the skull-faced man. "Room 101," he says. There is a gasp and a flurry at Winston's side. The man has actually flung himself on his knees on the floor, with his hands clasped together. "Comrade! Officer!" he cries. They don't have to take him to that place! Hasn't he told them everything already? What else is it they want to know? There's nothing he wouldn't confess, nothing! Just tell him what it is and he'll confess straight off. Write it down and he'll sign it — anything! Not room 101! "Room 101," says the officer. The man's face, already very pale, turns a colour Winston would not have believed possible. It is definitely, unmistakably, a shade of green. "Do anything to me!" he yells. They have been starving him for weeks. Finish it off and let him die. Shoot him. Hang him. Sentence him to twenty-five years. Is there somebody else they want him to give away? Just say who it is and he'll tell them anything they want. He doesn't care who it is or what they do to them. He has a wife and three children. The biggest of them isn't six years old. They can take the whole lot of them and cut their throats in front of his eyes, and he will stand by and watch it. But not Room 101! "Room 101," says the officer.
