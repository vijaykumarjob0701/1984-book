## Summary

The mess includes dog-eared school books, red Youth League and Spies banners, and a full-size Big Brother poster. Under the building's cabbage smell is a sharp reek of sweat that belongs to someone not in the room. In another room a child tries to play along with the telescreen's military music using a comb and toilet paper. Mrs Parsons glances nervously at the door and says the children have not been out. The sink is full of filthy green water. Winston kneels, hating the work and the way bending starts his cough. She praises absent Tom as good with his hands. Tom Parsons works with Winston at the Ministry of Truth: fat but energetic, almost stupidly loyal, the kind of eager drudge the Party relies on even more than the Thought Police. At thirty-five he has been pushed out of the Youth League after staying in the Spies past the legal age. His Ministry job needs no brains, but he leads sports and "voluntary" committees and boasts of attending the Community Centre every evening for four years. A powerful smell of sweat follows him as proof of that strenuous life.
