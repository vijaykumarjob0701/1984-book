## Summary

There is, of course, no admission that any change has taken place. Merely it becomes known, with extreme suddenness and everywhere at once, that Eastasia and not Eurasia is the enemy. Winston is taking part in a demonstration in one of the central London squares when it happens. It is night; white faces and scarlet banners are luridly floodlit. The square is packed with several thousand people, including a block of about a thousand schoolchildren in Spies uniform. On a scarlet-draped platform an Inner Party orator — a small lean man with disproportionately long arms and a large bald skull over which a few lank locks straggle — is haranguing the crowd. A little fairy-tale goblin figure, contorted with hatred, he grips the microphone with one hand while the other, enormous at the end of a bony arm, claws the air menacingly. His metallic amplified voice booms an endless catalogue of atrocities, massacres, deportations, lootings, rapes, torture of prisoners, bombing of civilians, lying propaganda, unjust aggressions, broken treaties. It is almost impossible to listen without being first convinced and then maddened. Every few moments the crowd's fury boils over and a wild beast-like roaring from thousands of throats drowns the speaker. The most savage yells come from the schoolchildren. The speech has been going perhaps twenty minutes when a messenger hurries onto the platform and a scrap of paper is slipped into the speaker's hand. He unrolls and reads it without pausing. Nothing alters in his voice or manner, or in the content of what he is saying, but suddenly the names are different. Without words said, a wave of understanding ripples through the crowd. Oceania is at war with Eastasia! The next moment there is a tremendous commotion. The banners and posters decorating the square are all wrong! Quite half of them have the wrong faces. It is sabotage! Goldstein's agents have been at work! There is a riotous interlude while posters are ripped from the walls, banners torn to shreds and trampled.
