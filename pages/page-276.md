## Summary

Translating a B-word into Oldspeak usually takes a long paraphrase and loses overtones. B-words are a tight verbal shorthand. They are always compounds (two or more bits welded into one noun-verb). GOODTHINK, roughly "orthodoxy," inflects in the regular way: GOODTHINKED, GOODTHINKFUL, GOODTHINKWISE, GOODTHINKER. The pieces can be any part of speech, in any order, chopped for ease of saying: CRIMETHINK versus THINKPOL (where POLICE loses a syllable). Awkward ministry adjectives become MINITRUTHFUL, MINIPEACEFUL, MINILOVELY because the "correct" forms sound worse. Some B-words are so loaded that only a fluent speaker feels them. A sample *Times* line is given: OLDTHINKERS UNBELLYFEEL INGSOC. The shortest Oldspeak rendering would be that people formed before the Revolution cannot fully feel English Socialism — and even that, the next page says, is not enough.
