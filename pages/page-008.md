## Summary

The Ministry of Love is the most terrifying: windowless, hard to approach, ringed by wire, steel doors, hidden guns, and brutal guards with jointed truncheons. Winston has never gone near it. He turns to the telescreen with a carefully hopeful face, then goes to a kitchen that has almost no food — only dark bread saved for breakfast — because he skipped the canteen lunch. He drinks a teacup of oily Victory Gin; it burns like acid and feels like a blow to the head, then the world seems brighter. He lights a Victory Cigarette, first spilling the tobacco. At a table beside the telescreen he takes out a pen, ink, and a thick blank book with a red spine and marbled cover. The living-room telescreen is on the long wall, not the end wall, so it cannot see the whole room. Winston sits in a shallow alcove that was probably meant for bookshelves.
