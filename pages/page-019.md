## Summary

Winston wonders whether underground plots are real and whether the Brotherhood exists. Endless arrests and confessions never prove it is not a myth. Some days he believes, some days not. Evidence is only scraps: overheard talk, lavatory graffiti, a possible hand signal between strangers. He probably imagined it. He goes back to his cubicle without looking at O'Brien again; following up would be unthinkably dangerous. The glance lasted a second or two, but in their locked loneliness even that is memorable. Back in the present he sits up, belches as the gin rises, and sees that while he was daydreaming his hand has kept writing. The script is no longer cramped. In large neat capitals he has filled half a page with "DOWN WITH BIG BROTHER" again and again. Panic flickers. Those words are no more dangerous than opening the diary, but he almost wants to tear the pages out and quit.
