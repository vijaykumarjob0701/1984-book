**Location:** Part Three, Chapter 3

## Summary

Winston stops sobbing and looks up. He has not betrayed Julia, he says. O'Brien agrees: that is perfectly true. The old reverence floods back — O'Brien always understands. Anyone else would have said he *had* betrayed her: under torture he has told every fact about her life and their meetings. In the sense he means, though, he has not. He still loves her; his feeling is unchanged, and O'Brien saw that without being told. Winston asks how soon they will shoot him. It might be a long time, O'Brien says; he is a difficult case, but everyone is cured sooner or later. In the end they shall shoot him. Chapter 3 ends there.
