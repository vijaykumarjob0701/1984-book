## Summary

O'Brien picks up from the fingernail image. The Party, he says, are priests of power, and power is what they mean by God. Power is collective: a person has it only by ceasing to be a separate self. The slogan *Freedom is Slavery* can be turned round — *Slavery is freedom* — because a lone human always dies, while someone who merges into the Party shares its immortality. The second lesson is that power is power over people, especially over minds, not over climate or rocks. Winston sits up enough to object: they do not even control gravity, disease, or the other two super-states. O'Brien waves that away. They control matter because they control the mind; they make the laws of Nature; Oceania is the world.
