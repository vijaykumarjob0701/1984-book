## Summary

...cheons on his elbows and iron-shod boots on his shins; he sees himself grovelling on the floor, screaming for mercy through broken teeth. He hardly thinks of Julia. He cannot fix his mind on her. He loves her and will not betray her; but that is only a fact, known as he knows the rules of arithmetic. He feels no love for her, and he hardly even wonders what is happening to her. He thinks oftener of O'Brien, with a flickering hope. O'Brien might know he has been arrested. The Brotherhood, he had said, never tried to save its members. But there was the razor blade; they would send the razor blade if they could. There would be perhaps five seconds before the guard could rush into the cell. The blade would bite into him with a sort of burning coldness, and even the fingers that held it would be cut to the bone. Everything comes back to his sick body, which shrinks trembling from the smallest pain. He is not certain he would use the razor blade even if he got the chance. It is more natural to exist from moment to moment, accepting another ten minutes' life even with the certainty that there is torture at the end of it. Sometimes he tries to calculate the number of porcelain bricks in the walls. It should be easy, but he always loses count. More often he wonders where he is, and what time of day it is. At one moment he feels certain it is broad daylight outside, and at the next equally certain it is pitch darkness. In this place, he knows instinctively, the lights will never be turned out. It is the place with no darkness: he sees now why O'Brien seemed to recognize the allusion. In the Ministry of Love there are no windows. His cell might be at the heart of the building or against its outer wall; it might be ten floors below ground, or thirty above it. He moves himself mentally from place to place and tries to determine by the feeling of his body whether he is perched high in the air or buried deep underground. There is a sound of marching boots outside. The steel door opens with a clang. A young officer, a trim black-uniformed figure who seems to glitter all over with polished leather, and whose pale, straight-featured face is like a wax mask, steps smartly through the doorway. He motions to the guards outside...
