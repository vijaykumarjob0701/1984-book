## Summary

The appendix quotes a stretch of the American Declaration of Independence (life, liberty, consent of the governed, the right to alter or abolish a government). That sense cannot be put into Newspeak at all. The nearest rendering is to swallow the whole passage in the single word CRIMETHINK. A "full" version would have to become praise of absolute government. Much older literature is already being rewritten this way. Famous names are kept for prestige, but their work is lined up with Ingsoc. Writers such as Shakespeare, Milton, Swift, Byron, and Dickens are said to be in translation; when that job is done, the originals — and the rest of the past — will be destroyed. The work is slow, not expected to finish before the early twenty-first century. Technical manuals need the same treatment. That is the main reason the final switch to Newspeak was set as late as 2050. The appendix ends with THE END.
