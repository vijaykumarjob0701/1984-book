## Summary

From about that time war has been continuous, though not always the same war. He remembers confused street fighting in London, but no written or spoken record ever admits any alliance except the present one. In 1984 (if it is 1984) Oceania is at war with Eurasia and allied with Eastasia. Nobody is allowed to say the three powers were ever grouped differently. Winston knows that only four years ago the partners were reversed, but that is furtive knowledge his memory has failed to control. Officially the switch never happened: Oceania is at war with Eurasia, therefore it always has been. Today's enemy is absolute evil, so any past or future deal with him is treated as impossible. While he painfully twists his torso in a back exercise, the frightening thought returns: it might all be true. If the Party can put its hand into the past and say an event never happened, that is worse than torture or death. The Party says there was never an alliance with Eurasia; he knows there was, but the knowledge lives only in his mind, which will soon be destroyed. If everyone accepts the lie and all records agree, the lie becomes history. The slogan says who controls the past controls the future, and who controls the present controls the past.
