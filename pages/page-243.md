**PDF page:** 243  
**Location:** Part Three, Chapter 3

## Summary

O'Brien drops the astronomy lecture. The power they fight for is over men, not objects. How does one person force another? Winston answers: by making him suffer. Exactly, says O'Brien — obedience without pain might still be the victim's own will. The world they are building is the reverse of old reformers' pleasure-dreams: more fear, treachery, and pain as the system gets "better." Their civilisation is founded on hatred. Family, friendship, sex, art, literature, and science will go. Children will be taken at birth; even the orgasm is to be abolished. The only allowed love will be love of Big Brother. The page breaks as he says competing pleasures will be destroyed, but always —
