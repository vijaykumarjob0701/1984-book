## Summary

The scene must have been about a month before his mother disappeared: a wet day, hungry, bored children, neighbours banging the wall. She goes out in the rain and brings Snakes and Ladders in damp cardboard — a miserable set, cracked board, badly cut dice. Then she lights a candle and they play on the floor. He shouts with laughter as the pieces climb ladders and slide snakes. Eight games, four each; his tiny sister laughs because they laugh. For a whole afternoon they were happy. He pushes the picture away. It is a false memory; some things happened, others did not. He picks up the white knight. It clatters onto the board. A shrill trumpet has pierced the air. The bulletin. Victory. It always means victory when a trumpet comes first.
