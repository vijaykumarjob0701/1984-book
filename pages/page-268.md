## Summary

The office is in the Ministry of Truth. He sits on a sub-committee of a sub-committee about the Eleventh Edition of the Newspeak Dictionary — an Interim Report whose subject he has never quite pinned down, something about commas inside brackets. Four others like himself. Some days they meet and leave, admitting there is nothing to do. Other days they fake huge energy: minutes, unfinished memoranda, haggling, threats to appeal — then the life goes out and they look at one another with extinct eyes, like ghosts at cock-crow. The telescreen is only changing music, not yet the bulletin. Behind his eyelids: a black arrow south through Africa, a white arrow east across its tail. He looks at the imperturbable portrait. Could the second arrow not even exist? Interest flags. He drinks, moves the white knight: check — wrong. Uncalled, a memory: candle-light, a vast white bed, himself aged nine or ten shaking a dice-box, his mother laughing opposite.
