## Summary

...lived. There are no directories of any kind. The real message is simply where he can be found. Perhaps there will even be a message concealed in the dictionary. At any rate one thing is certain. The conspiracy Winston has dreamed of does exist, and he has reached the outer edges of it. He knows that sooner or later he will obey O'Brien's summons. Perhaps tomorrow, perhaps after a long delay — he is not certain. What is happening is only the working-out of a process that started years ago. The first step was a secret, involuntary thought; the second was the opening of the diary. He has moved from thoughts to words, and now from words to actions. The last step is something that will happen in the Ministry of Love. He has accepted it. The end was contained in the beginning. But it is frightening: or, more exactly, it is like a foretaste of death, like being a little less alive. Even while he was speaking to O'Brien, when the meaning sank in, a chilly shuddering feeling took possession of his body. He had the sensation of stepping into the dampness of a grave, and it was not much better because he had always known that the grave was there and waiting for him. Chapter 6 ends on that feeling.
