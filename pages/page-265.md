## Summary

They could have met again if either had wanted. The actual meeting was chance: a bitter March day in the Park, dead grass, a few crocuses ruined by wind. He almost walks past, then follows. She tries to angle away, then lets him walk beside her. They stop among ragged shrubs that hide nothing. He puts an arm round her waist. There is no telescreen, but there will be microphones; it does not matter. His flesh freezes at the thought of more. She does not respond or pull away. The change is not only the scar on her forehead. Her waist is thicker and stiff, like the rigid corpse he once dragged from ruins. They do not kiss or speak. Crossing the grass she gives him one glance, full of contempt and dislike.
