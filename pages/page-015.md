## Summary

Behind Goldstein the telescreen shows endless columns of Eurasian soldiers with blank faces, their boots a dull beat under his voice. Within thirty seconds people shout in rage. Goldstein is a more constant hate-object than Eurasia or Eastasia, because Oceania is usually at war with only one of them at a time. Strangely, though he is mocked every day in every medium, his influence never seems to shrink; there are always new "dupes," and the Thought Police always "unmask" spies said to follow him. He is imagined as leader of a shadowy underground called the Brotherhood, and as author of a nameless forbidden book people call THE BOOK. Ordinary Party members avoid speaking of either. In the second minute the Hate becomes a frenzy: people leap and scream to drown the voice on the screen.
