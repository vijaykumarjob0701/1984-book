**Location:** Part Three, Chapter 3

## Summary

Winston's reason is that a civilisation based on fear, hatred, and cruelty would have no vitality; it would fall apart. O'Brien calls that nonsense. Hatred need not tire people more than love; even if it did, the Party does not die when a person does. Winston, fearing the dial but unable to be silent, says life will defeat them. O'Brien answers that they control life and create human nature; people are infinitely malleable. The proles will not rise; they are like animals, outside "humanity," which is the Party. Winston still "knows" they will fail. There is some spirit or principle they cannot beat. O'Brien asks if he believes in God. Winston says no.
