## Summary

Chapter 5: Winston has always had a sense of height in the windowless building — beating-cells below ground, O'Brien's interrogation room high up. This place is as deep as the building goes. He barely sees the room. Two small tables covered in green baize stand in front of him. He is strapped upright so tightly he cannot move his head; a pad forces his eyes forward. O'Brien enters and reminds him of the old question: what is in Room 101? Everyone knows. It is the worst thing in the world. A guard sets a wire box on the further table. The worst thing, O'Brien goes on, differs from person to person — fire, drowning, even some trivial fear. He steps aside. The object is an oblong cage with a mask-like front, split into two compartments. In each is a rat.
