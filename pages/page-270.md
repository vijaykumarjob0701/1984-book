## Summary

An electric shock runs through the café; even waiters listen. Cheering from the street almost drowns the telescreen. It has happened as he foresaw: a seaborne armada in the enemy's rear, the white arrow across the black. Phrases break through the din — vast manoeuvre, half a million prisoners, control of Africa, the greatest victory in human history. Under the table his feet twitch; in his mind he is running with the crowd, cheering himself deaf. He looks at Big Brother: the colossus, the rock Asia dashes against in vain. Ten minutes ago there was still equivocation about victory or defeat. More than an army has perished. The final healing change, missing since the Ministry of Love, happens now. The voice still talks of booty. Winston sits in a blissful dream. He is back in the Ministry, forgiven, soul white as snow, in the dock confessing, then walking the white-tiled corridor.
