## Summary

Fifteen-thirty: music resumes. His heart stirs. Instinct says the African news will be a smash. He sees the Eurasian army pouring down the continent like ants. Why could they not be outflanked? He moves the white knight and, in his mind, plants a force in the enemy's rear. If Africa falls, Oceania could be cut in two — even the Party destroyed. Layers of feeling struggle, then the spasm passes. He cannot study the problem. Almost without noticing, he traces in the dust: 2+2=5. Julia had said they cannot get inside you. They could. O'Brien had said what happens here is for ever: something in the breast is burnt out. He has seen her; he has even spoken to her. There is no danger. They take almost no interest in him now.
