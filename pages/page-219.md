## Summary

...are no heroes, no heroes, he thinks over and over as he writhes on the floor, clutching uselessly at his disabled left arm. The page is short: the first chapter of Part Three ends on that thought. One blow has already taught him that heroism does not survive this kind of pain. Chapter 1 closes with Winston on the floor of the cell, O'Brien and the guard looking down, and the lesson still repeating in his head.
