## Summary

The story is over. This appendix is an in-world essay on Newspeak, Oceania's official language, built for Ingsoc. In 1984 nobody yet speaks only Newspeak; *The Times* leaders in it are a specialist stunt. The plan is that Newspeak will have replaced Oldspeak (ordinary English) by about 2050. The 1984 dictionaries (Ninth and Tenth Editions) are still full of leftover words; the essay describes the final, Eleventh Edition version. The aim is not only to express Party thought but to make every other kind of thought impossible once Oldspeak is forgotten. New words help, but the main work is killing unwanted words and stripping the rest of extra meanings. Example begun here: FREE still exists, but only in sentences such as a dog being free from lice.
