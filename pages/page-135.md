## Summary

...the feeling that he could get inside the paperweight, and that in fact he is inside it, along with the mahogany bed and the gateleg table, the clock, the steel engraving, and the paperweight itself. The paperweight is the room he is in, and the coral is Julia's life and his own, fixed in a sort of eternity at the heart of the crystal. Chapter 4 ends on that image.
