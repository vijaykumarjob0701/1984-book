## Summary

He feels utterly alone, as if on a sunlit desert, though the cage is not two metres away. The rats are huge, blunt-muzzled, brown. O'Brien, still speaking to an invisible audience, says the rat is a meat-eater. In poor streets a woman dare not leave a baby; the rats will strip it, and they attack the sick. Squeals: the rats fight through the partition. Winston hears a groan that seems to come from outside himself. O'Brien presses something; a click. Winston cannot tear free. The cage is less than a metre from his face. The mask will fit with no exit. The second lever will slide the door; the starving animals will leap, go for the eyes, or burrow through the cheeks. The cage closes in. The musty smell hits. Nausea.
