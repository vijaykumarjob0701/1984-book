## Summary

Almost without thinking he crumples the original slips and his notes and drops them into the memory hole to be burned. He does not know the tube system in detail, but he knows the pattern. When all "corrections" for an issue of *The Times* are gathered and compared, that issue is reprinted, the old copy destroyed, and the new one filed in its place. The same endless rewriting hits books, magazines, pamphlets, posters, leaflets, films, sound-tracks, cartoons, photographs — anything with political meaning. Minute by minute the past is brought up to date, so every Party prediction can be "proved" and no inconvenient news or opinion survives. History is a palimpsest, scraped clean and written over as often as needed. Afterward nobody could prove a falsification. A far larger Records section only hunts down superseded copies for burning. A *Times* issue rewritten a dozen times still sits on file under its original date, with no rival copy. Books are recalled and reissued as if never changed. Even Winston's instructions never admit forgery.
