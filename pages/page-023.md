## Summary

The sweat-smell lingers even after Parsons has gone. Winston asks for a spanner; Mrs Parsons goes limp and vague. The children stamp in, blasting the comb again. She finds the tool. Winston drains the sink and pulls out a clot of hair, then rinses his fingers in cold water. A tough-looking nine-year-old boy in Spies uniform jumps up with a toy automatic and yells "Up with your hands!" His younger sister copies him with a stick. Both wear blue shorts, grey shirts, and red neckerchiefs. Winston raises his hands, but the boy's manner is so vicious it does not feel wholly like play. They shout that he is a traitor, a thought-criminal, a Eurasian spy, and threaten shooting, vaporizing, and the salt mines. They leap around him like tiger cubs that will grow into man-eaters. The boy looks eager to hit him and almost big enough. Winston is glad the pistol is a toy. Mrs Parsons's eyes flick between them; in better light Winston sees there really is dust in the creases of her face.
