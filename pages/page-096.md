## Summary

He cannot follow the thought further. He puts a cigarette in his mouth; half the tobacco falls onto his tongue as a bitter dust that is hard to spit out. Big Brother's face swims into his mind and displaces O'Brien. As he did a few days earlier, he slides a coin from his pocket and looks at it. The face gazes up, heavy, calm, protecting — but what kind of smile is hidden under the dark moustache? Like a leaden knell the three slogans come back: war is peace, freedom is slavery, ignorance is strength. Chapter 8, and Part One, end on that returning chant.
