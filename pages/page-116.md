## Summary

...quite come back. He pulls the overalls aside and studies the smooth white flank. In the old days, he thinks, a man looked at a girl's body, saw that it was desirable, and that was the end of the story. Now you cannot have pure love or pure lust. No emotion is pure, because everything is mixed up with fear and hatred. Their embrace has been a battle, the climax a victory. It was a blow struck against the Party. It was a political act. Chapter 2 ends on that thought.
