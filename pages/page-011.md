## Summary

In a panic Winston starts writing, barely knowing what he puts down. His childish handwriting loses capital letters and then full stops. He records last night's cinema: all war films. The audience laughs at a fat refugee in the sea being machine-gunned from a helicopter until the water turns pink. Then a lifeboat of children is bombed; a frightened woman tries to shield a small boy with her arms. A child's arm is filmed flying upward, and Party seats applaud. A woman in the prole section shouts that they should not have shown it in front of children, until police throw her out. Winston notes that nobody much cares what proles say, then his sentence breaks off.
