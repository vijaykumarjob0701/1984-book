## Summary

In that instant he loves her more than when they were free, and he knows she is still alive and needs him. He tries to compose himself: how many years has that cry added to his sentence? They will have heard it. He obeys the Party but still hates it. Once he hid a heretical mind under a loyal face; now he had hoped to surrender the mind and keep the inner heart. The cry confessed that hope. He will have to start again. His face has new teeth and new hollows; inscrutability is harder when you do not know your own features. He sees a further rule: to keep a secret you must hide it from yourself. He must think, feel, and dream right, and lock hatred inside like a cyst. One day they will decide to shoot him from behind. Ten seconds would be enough for the inner world to turn over.
