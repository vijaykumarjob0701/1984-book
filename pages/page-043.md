# PDF page 043

**PDF page:** 43  
**Location:** Part One, Chapter 4

## Summary

In ordinary English the slip means: Big Brother's 3 December 1983 Order for the Day refers to people who do not exist; rewrite it fully and send a draft upstairs before filing. Winston reads the article. The Order praised FFCC, which sent cigarettes and comforts to Floating Fortress sailors, and specially honoured Inner Party member Comrade Withers with the Order of Conspicuous Merit, Second Class. Three months later FFCC was dissolved with no reason given. Withers and his people are presumably in disgrace, but nothing was reported. That is normal: political offenders are rarely tried in public. Huge show-trial purges happen only every couple of years; more often people who displease the Party simply vanish. You never get a clue; some may not even be dead. About thirty people Winston has known, besides his parents, have disappeared. He strokes his nose with a paper-clip. Tillotson is still crouched over his speakwrite and flashes another hostile look. Winston wonders if they have the same job. Work this tricky would not go to one person, but a committee would admit fabrication. Perhaps a dozen people are writing rival versions of what Big Brother "said," and some Inner Party master mind will pick one, re-edit it, and start the cross-referencing.
