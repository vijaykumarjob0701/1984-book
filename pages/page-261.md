## Summary

Everything goes black. For an instant he is a screaming animal, then he comes out clutching one idea: he must put another human body between himself and the rats. The mask already shuts out the rest of the room. One rat leaps; an old sewer grandfather stands on pink hands, sniffing. O'Brien remarks, as if giving a lesson, that this was a common punishment in Imperial China. Wire brushes Winston's cheek. Too late, perhaps — but he understands there is one person onto whom he can transfer the punishment. He shouts it over and over: do it to Julia, not me; he does not care what they do to her. He falls backwards through floor, earth, oceans, stars, always away from the rats, yet O'Brien is still beside him and the wire still cold on his face. Through the dark he hears another click. The cage door has shut, not opened. Chapter 5 ends.
