## Summary

Winston turns the Hate onto the dark-haired girl and is flooded with violent sexual fantasies of beating, shooting, and killing her. He now sees why he hates her: she is young and pretty yet "sexless," he wants her and knows he cannot have her, and the scarlet sash around her waist is an aggressive badge of chastity. The Hate peaks. Goldstein's voice becomes a sheep's bleat and his face a sheep, then a huge Eurasian soldier seems to leap from the screen with a roaring gun; people in front flinch. The figure melts into vast, calm Big Brother. Nobody catches his words — only a battle-noise of encouragement. His face fades into the three Party slogans again, but the image seems to stay on people's eyes. The sandy-haired woman throws herself forward, stretches her arms at the screen, and murmurs something like "My Saviour!"
