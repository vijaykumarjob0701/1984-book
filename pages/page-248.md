## Summary

The tuft of hair is only the start. O'Brien makes him open his mouth, counts the remaining teeth, and wrenches a loose front tooth out by the roots. Winston is rotting, he says — a bag of filth; if this is a man, this is humanity; now dress. While tying the rags Winston realises he must have been here longer than he thought, then pity for his own body breaks him: he collapses on a stool and weeps. O'Brien's hand on his shoulder is almost kind. It will not last for ever; he can escape whenever he chooses. Winston sobs that *they* reduced him to this. No, says O'Brien: you did it when you set yourself against the Party. They have beaten him in body and mind; he has screamed, betrayed everyone — can he think of a single degradation that has not happened?
