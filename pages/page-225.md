## Summary

...fall below your usual level of intelligence, you will cry out with pain, instantly. Do you understand that? Yes, says Winston. O'Brien's manner becomes less severe. He resettles his spectacles thoughtfully and takes a pace or two up and down. When he speaks his voice is gentle and patient. He has the air of a doctor, a teacher, even a priest, anxious to explain and persuade rather than to punish. He is taking trouble with Winston because Winston is worth trouble. Winston knows perfectly well what is the matter with him. He has known it for years, though he has fought against the knowledge. He is mentally deranged. He suffers from a defective memory. He is unable to remember real events and he persuades himself that he remembers other events which never happened. Fortunately it is curable. He has never cured himself of it, because he did not choose to. There was a small effort of the will that he was not ready to make. Even now, O'Brien is well aware, he is clinging to his disease under the impression that it is a virtue. Now they will take an example. At this moment, which power is Oceania at war with? When Winston was arrested, Oceania was at war with Eastasia. With Eastasia. Good. And Oceania has always been at war with Eastasia, has it not? Winston draws in his breath. He opens his mouth to speak and then does not speak. He cannot take his eyes away from the dial. The truth, please, Winston. *Your* truth. Tell me what you think you remember. He remembers that until only a week before he was arrested, they were not at war with Eastasia at all. They were in alliance with them. The war was against Eurasia. That had lasted for four years. Before that —— O'Brien stops him with a movement of the hand. Another example. Some years ago Winston had a very serious delusion indeed. He believed that three men, three one-time Party members named Jones, Aaronson, and Rutherford — men who were executed for treachery and sabotage after making the fullest possible confession — were not guilty of the crimes they were charged with. | deranged | mentally ill (O'Brien's word for Winston's loyalty to facts) |
