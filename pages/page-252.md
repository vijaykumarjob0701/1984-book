## Summary

For seven years, he now accepts, the Thought Police watched him like a beetle under glass: every act, every word, even the speck of dust they replaced on the diary. Photographs of Julia and himself exist. He cannot fight any longer, and besides the Party must be right — an immortal collective brain cannot be checked by an outside standard. Sanity is statistical: learn to think as they think. On the slate, in clumsy capitals, he writes FREEDOM IS SLAVERY, then TWO AND TWO MAKE FIVE. The third line will not come until he reasons it out: GOD IS POWER. He accepts the rest. The past is alterable and was never altered. Oceania is at war with Eastasia and always has been. Jones, Aaronson, and Rutherford were guilty; the photograph never existed. Contrary memories are self-deception.
