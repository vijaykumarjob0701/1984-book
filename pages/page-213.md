## Summary

...ated brings in a powerful smell of cold sweat. Parsons walks into the cell. He is wearing khaki shorts and a sports-shirt. This time Winston is startled into self-forgetfulness. "YOU here!" he says. Parsons gives him a glance in which there is neither interest nor surprise, but only misery. He begins walking jerkily up and down, evidently unable to keep still. Each time he straightens his pudgy knees it is apparent that they are trembling. His eyes have a wide-open, staring look, as though he cannot prevent himself from gazing at something in the middle distance. What is he in for? "Thoughtcrime!" says Parsons, almost blubbering. The tone implies at once a complete admission of guilt and a sort of incredulous horror that such a word could be applied to himself. He pauses opposite Winston and begins eagerly appealing to him. Winston doesn't think they will shoot him, does he, old chap? They don't shoot you if you haven't actually done anything — only thoughts, which you can't help? He knows they give you a fair hearing. Oh, he trusts them for that! They'll know his record, won't they? Winston knows what kind of chap he was. Not a bad chap in his way. Not brainy, of course, but keen. He tried to do his best for the Party, didn't he? He'll get off with five years, don't you think? Or even ten? A chap like him could make himself pretty useful in a labour-camp. They wouldn't shoot him for going off the rails just once? Is he guilty? "Of course I'm guilty!" cries Parsons with a servile glance at the telescreen. You don't think the Party would arrest an innocent man, do you? His frog-like face grows calmer, and even takes on a slightly sanctimonious expression. Thoughtcrime is a dreadful thing, old man. It's insidious. It can get hold of you without your even knowing it. Does Winston know how it got hold of him? In his sleep! Yes, that's a fact. There he was, working away, trying to do his bit — never knew he had any bad stuff in his mind at all. And then he started talking in his sleep. Does Winston know what they heard him saying? He sinks his voice, like someone obliged for medical reasons to utter an obscenity.
