## Summary

...it is almost impossible to catch a single word. Once Winston hears "complete and final elimination of Goldsteinism," jerked out like a solid line of type. The rest is quack-quack. You still know the general nature: attacks on Goldstein, harsher measures, Eurasian atrocities, praise of Big Brother or the Malabar heroes — it makes no difference; every word is pure orthodoxy. Watching the eyeless, rapidly chewing jaw, Winston feels this is a dummy: not a brain speaking but a larynx. The output is words without real speech, a noise uttered in unconsciousness, like a duck. Syme traces patterns in leftover stew. He names a Newspeak word, duckspeak: to quack like a duck. It has two opposite uses — abuse for an opponent, praise for someone you agree with. Winston again thinks Syme will be vaporized, sadly, though Syme despises him and would denounce him if he had a reason. Something is subtly wrong with Syme: he lacks discretion, distance, a saving stupidity. He is not unorthodox. He believes Ingsoc, worships Big Brother, loves victories, hates heretics with sincere, restless, up-to-date zeal that ordinary members cannot match.
