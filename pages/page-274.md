## Summary

Any word can in principle be verb, noun, adjective, or adverb. THINK covers both the act and the idea; THOUGHT as a separate form is gone. CUT disappears because KNIFE already covers it. Adjectives take *-FUL*, adverbs *-WISE*: SPEEDFUL, SPEEDWISE. WELL becomes GOODWISE. Any word can be negated with UN- or boosted with PLUS- and DOUBLEPLUS- (UNCOLD, PLUSCOLD, DOUBLEPLUSCOLD). Prefixes such as ANTE- and POST- do more of the work. That is how the word-list shrinks. GOOD makes BAD unnecessary: UNGOOD is better, the essay says, because it is an exact opposite. When two words are a natural pair, you only keep one.
