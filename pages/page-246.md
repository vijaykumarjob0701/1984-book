## Summary

The principle, Winston says, is the spirit of Man. O'Brien tells him that if he is a man he is the last one: his kind is extinct; he is alone and outside history. Then the tone hardens: does Winston still think himself morally better than their lies and cruelty? Winston says yes. A recording plays — Winston's own Brotherhood oath, including the worst promises. O'Brien barely bothers with it, then orders him off the bed. The straps have loosened. "You are the last man," O'Brien repeats; take off your clothes and look. Winston strips filthy rags and sees a three-sided mirror. He stops with an involuntary cry. A bowed, grey, skeleton-like thing is coming toward him.
