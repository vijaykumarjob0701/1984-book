## Summary

Without a change of step or face, the camouflage would drop and hatred would roar — and in the same instant the bullet would come, too late or too early for them to reclaim his brain. To die hating them: that would be freedom. He shuts his eyes. Loving Big Brother will be harder than an intellectual drill; he must degrade himself. The huge poster-face floats up. What are his true feelings? Boots in the passage. O'Brien enters with the officer and guards. Winston has had thoughts of deceiving him; that was stupid. Intellectually he is almost well; emotionally he has failed. No lies: what are his true feelings towards Big Brother? "I hate him." Good, says O'Brien. Then the last step: he must love Big Brother. Obedience is not enough. A little push toward the guards.
