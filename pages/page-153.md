## Summary

...mastered the secret of finding out what another human being was thinking. Perhaps that is less true when you are actually in their hands. One does not know what happens inside the Ministry of Love, but it is possible to guess: tortures, drugs, delicate instruments that register nervous reactions, gradual wearing-down by sleeplessness and solitude and persistent questioning. Facts, at any rate, cannot be kept hidden. They can be tracked down by enquiry; they can be squeezed out of you by torture. But if the object is not to stay alive but to stay human, what difference does it ultimately make? They cannot alter your feelings: for that matter you cannot alter them yourself, even if you wanted to. They could lay bare in the utmost detail everything you had done or said or thought; but the inner heart, whose workings were mysterious even to yourself, remained impregnable. Chapter 7 ends on that belief.
