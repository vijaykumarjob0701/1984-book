## Summary

Ease of sound outranks even regular grammar when they clash. What politics needs is short, clipped words that can be fired off fast and raise almost no echoes. Most B-words are two or three syllables with even stress, and they sound alike: GOODTHINK, MINIPAX, PROLEFEED, SEXCRIME, JOYCAMP, INGSOC, BELLYFEEL, THINKPOL. That sameness encourages a gabbling, machine-like delivery. A Party member making a political judgement should spray correct opinions as automatically as a gun sprays bullets. Harsh, ugly texture is part of the design. The vocabulary is tiny and still shrinking; each cut is a gain because there is less room to stop and think. The hope is that speech might one day leave the throat without using the higher brain at all. DUCKSPEAK ("to quack like a duck") names that aim. Like other B-words it is two-faced: orthodox quacking is praise.
